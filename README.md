# Fish Dot 🐟

A cozy two-player online game. Drop a dot, fold the paper, eat the fish!

**Play:** https://naman9245.github.io/fish-dot-game/

## How to play

1. One player taps **Create Game** and shares the link (or the room code) with their partner.
2. 10 fish are scattered all over the paper, in new spots every game.
3. On your turn, tap anywhere to drop a nail-polish dot.
4. The paper folds in half and your dot prints at the mirror spot on the other side.
5. If the print lands on a fish, you eat it. When every fish is eaten, whoever ate the most wins.
6. On a phone, play with it turned sideways: the paper is wide.

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Live sync between the two phones uses Supabase Realtime.
