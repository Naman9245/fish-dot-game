How to play?
  1. 🐟 10 fish are hiding all over the paper.
  2. 💅 On your turn, tap anywhere to drop a nail-polish dot.
  3. 📄 The paper folds in half, and your dot prints on the other side at the mirror spot.
  4. 😋 If the print lands on a fish, you eat it!
  5. 🏆 When every fish is eaten, whoever ate the most wins.
  6. 📱 On a phone, play with it turned sideways.
